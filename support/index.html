Frequently asked

Notifications aren't arriving

Make sure Perday's notification permission is enabled in your phone settings. On some Android devices battery optimization can delay notifications — set Settings → Battery → Perday to "unrestricted". For exact-time reminders, approve the "alarms & reminders" permission when the app asks.

How do I secure my guest account?

Tap the "Secure your account" card on the Profile tab and link with Google, Apple or email. Your data is kept exactly as is — and you can now reach it from any device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the store: Google Play subscriptions · App Store subscriptions. After cancelling, Plus access continues until the end of the current period.

How do I delete my data?

In the app via Profile → "Delete account", or by email. Step by step: account deletion page.
