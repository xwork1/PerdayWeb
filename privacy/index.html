Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Perday ("the app") is developed and published by Appouse. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and your rights. Questions: info@appouse.com

Data we collect

  1. Account information. Your email address when you sign up with email; the name, email address and profile picture shared by the provider when you sign in with Google or Apple. In guest (anonymous) mode no identity data is collected; an anonymous user ID is assigned to your device.
  2. App data. The habits you create, completion records, streaks, badges, friend connections, challenge memberships and notification preferences — stored on our servers so the app can work across devices.
  3. Purchase data. Your subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. Your payment details (card number etc.) never reach us; payments are processed by the App Store / Google Play.
  4. Diagnostics. When the app crashes, the device model, OS version and crash log are sent to Firebase Crashlytics.
  5. Notification identifier. Your device's FCM token and app language are stored for push notifications.
What we do not collect: location, contacts, advertising identifiers. Perday shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics beyond Crashlytics.

How we use data

Only to: provide the service (habit tracking and sync across devices), verify premium access, send notifications, and fix bugs. We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties other than the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase — authentication, push notifications, crash reporting
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Our hosting provider — app server and database

Each provider is governed by its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by emailing us; deletion permanently removes all your data including habits, completions, badges, friendships and subscription records. Details: account deletion page.

Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable law you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Send requests to info@appouse.com.

Age

Perday is a habit-tracking and reminder app suitable for all ages; no age restriction applies. The app contains no ads, and no user's data is processed for advertising or profiling purposes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page; significant changes will also be announced in the app.
