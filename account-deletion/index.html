Deleting your account and data

Last updated: July 10, 2026

You can permanently delete your Perday account and all data linked to it at any time.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Perday and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap "Delete account" at the bottom of the settings section.
  3. Confirm in the dialog. For security you may be asked to re-enter your password.

If you can't access the app

Send an email with the subject "account deletion" from the email address registered to your account to info@appouse.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What gets deleted?

  • Account and identity information
  • All habits, completion records and streaks
  • Badges, XP and level progress
  • Friendships and challenge memberships
  • Notification records and device tokens
  • Subscription entitlement records
Important: Deletion cannot be undone. If you have an active Perday Premium subscription, deleting the account does not cancel it automatically — cancel the subscription separately in your App Store / Google Play subscription settings.

Deleted data is fully purged from backups within 30 days at the latest.
